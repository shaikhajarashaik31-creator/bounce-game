❓ HOW TO PLAY

🎮 CONTROLS

  • Left / Right: ◀ ▶ Arrow keys, A / D, or Nokia keys 4 / 6
  • Jump / Bounce: ▲ Arrow, W, Space, or Nokia keys 2 / 5
  • Down: ▼ Arrow, S, or Nokia key 8
  • Pause / Menu: P, Escape, or Left Soft Key

⭕ OBJECTIVES & MECHANICS

  • Golden Rings: Collect ALL rings in each level to unlock the Exit Gate!
  • Exit Gate: Locked by default. Once rings are 0, jump inside to clear the level!
  • Spikes: Lethal red spikes pop your ball! Avoid at all costs.
  • Trampolines: Orange spring pads catapult you high into the sky!
  • Size Pumps:
    • Yellow (▲): Giant ball! Super bouncy, breaks cracked walls, floats on water!
    • Blue (▼): Tiny ball! Squeezes through 1-tile gaps and pipes!
    • Green (●): Returns ball to Normal size!
  • Water Buoyancy: Big ball floats effortlessly. Small ball sinks. Tap jump to swim upward!
